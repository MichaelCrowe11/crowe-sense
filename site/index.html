Built on a method, not a guess.

Most monitoring tells you a number and leaves you to interpret it. Crowe Sense measures every reading against a documented growing method: a written manual of more than a quarter million words and years of filmed grows, covering colonization timelines, fruiting conditions, and expected yields for each species.

That is the difference. The system does not just say the carbon dioxide is 900 parts per million. It knows that for lion's mane in fruiting, that is inside the band, that the room is on roughly day nine, and what the first flush should weigh on a block that size.
